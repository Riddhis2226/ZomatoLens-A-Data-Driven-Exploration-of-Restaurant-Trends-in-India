# 📊 Level 2 – Geo & Service-Based Trends Dashboard

Welcome to the **Level 2 Dashboard** of my Data Analysis Internship at **Cognifyz Technologies**. This dashboard explores geo-based and service-based patterns from a real-world restaurant dataset using powerful visualizations and interactive insights.

---

## ✅ Objective

To analyze and visualize trends in restaurant data across:

* Rating distributions
* Cuisine combinations
* Geographical clustering
* Chain popularity

---

---

## 📌 Key Features

### 📊 Task 1: Rating Distribution & Insights

* Histogram & Boxplot of Aggregate Ratings
* Rating Category Distribution
* Average Votes Calculation

### 🍽️ Task 2: Cuisine Combinations by Rating

* Top cuisine pairs with highest ratings
* Filtered cuisines with sufficient sample size (20+ entries)

### 🗺️ Task 3: Geographical Trends

* Interactive national map with restaurant ratings
* Zoomed-in city clusters (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore)

### 🏪 Task 4: Popular Restaurant Chains

* Most popular chains by number of branches, average rating, and total votes

### 📥 Download Processed Data

* One-click CSV export of cleaned and analyzed data
